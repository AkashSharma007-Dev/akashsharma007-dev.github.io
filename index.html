How a project runs

You see progress every week, and nothing is billed that was not agreed in writing.

  1. A free call

    Twenty minutes to understand what you need and whether I am the right fit.

  2. A fixed quote

    Scope, price and dates in writing. Half is paid to start, half on delivery.

  3. Weekly previews

    A live link every week, with two rounds of changes included.

  4. Handover

    Your files, your logins and a short guide. Fixes are free for 30 days.
