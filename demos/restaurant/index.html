chhaunknoun, Hindi. Say it "chh-ownk".

Whole spices bloomed in very hot ghee, then poured over a finished dish. It is the last step in most Indian home cooking, and the loudest one.

The table chhaunk

Order any dal or curry and it arrives unfinished. A cook brings a small iron pan to your table and does the last step in front of you.

  1. Ghee is heated in the pan until it shimmers.
  2. Cumin, mustard seed, dried chilli and garlic go in and crackle for ten seconds.
  3. The whole pan is poured over your dish, and the smell reaches the next table before the sound stops.
