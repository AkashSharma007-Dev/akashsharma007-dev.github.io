Questions

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. You get 14 days with every feature. If you do not pick a plan at the end, your account pauses and nothing is charged.

Can I bring my customers across from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file and match the columns. Most people are set up in under ten minutes.

How do card payments work?

Every invoice has a pay link. The money reaches your bank account two working days later, minus 1.5% + 20p per payment.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Export every customer, quote and invoice as CSV and PDF at any time, including after you cancel.
