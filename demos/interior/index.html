How a project runs

A 1,200 sq ft home usually takes 14 to 16 weeks from first visit to handover.

  1. Site visit

    We measure, photograph and listen. You get a brief to approve.

    Week 1

  2. Layouts and 3D

    Two layout options, then 3D views of every room.

    Weeks 2 to 3

  3. Estimate

    An itemised estimate and a payment schedule tied to stages.

    Week 4

  4. Build

    Weekly site photos and one visit with you every fortnight.

    Weeks 5 to 14

  5. Handover

    Deep clean, snag list closed, and a folder of every warranty.

    Final week
