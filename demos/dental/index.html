What happens at your first visit

Plan for about 45 minutes. Bring any old X-rays or reports if you have them.

  1. Check in

    A short form about your health and any medicines you take.

    5 minutes

  2. Examination

    The dentist checks every tooth and your gums, and takes a digital X-ray if needed.

    20 minutes

  3. Your plan

    We show you the X-ray, explain the options and write down the cost of each.

    15 minutes

  4. Treatment

    Simple work can start the same day. Anything bigger is booked when it suits you.
